## MATH-500

The Index used **temperature 1.0**, Qwen’s recommendation for text tasks with thinking enabled. The patch authors used **0.6**. Both evaluations enabled thinking and capped replies at 32,768 tokens. [Qwen model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B/blob/2fc06364715b967f1860aea9cf38778875588b17/README.md#best-practices) · [Index settings](../../../../settings/qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking.json) · [Authors’ protocol](https://github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs/blob/4d09ba6d6a7e891dd8063974dfe81b3d78e3494f/exp8_math/code/evaluate.py)

The patch reduced token-limit hits in both evaluations. In the first **110 filed Index pairs**, the share fell from **80.0% to 64.5%**, alongside a **7.3 percentage-point** gain in correct final answers. The authors reported **93.0% to 33.1%** and a **40.2 percentage-point** gain in accuracy. Every correct Index grade in that snapshot came from a reply that finished within the cap. [Index base run](../../../../../models/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B/runs/math500-n197.qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking.bf16.rikiyat-thinking-loops-v1/run.json) · [Index patch run](../../../../../models/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B/rikiyat-thinking-loops/v1/runs/math500-n197.qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking.bf16/run.json) · [Authors’ results](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/#make-a-language-model-better-at-math)

The Index used one sample per item on a frozen 197-item subset. The authors averaged four samples per problem across all 500. The inference setups also differed. The Index grades the final boxed answer after thinking closes. The authors’ scorer also searches unfinished replies for a boxed answer. Measuring temperature’s contribution requires comparing both temperatures on the same items and seeds with the same engine and scoring.
