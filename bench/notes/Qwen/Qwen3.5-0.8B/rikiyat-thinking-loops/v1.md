## MATH-500

The Index’s main run used **temperature 1.0**, Qwen’s recommendation for text tasks with thinking enabled. The patch authors used **0.6**, so the Index also ran the patch at 0.6. All runs enabled thinking and capped replies at 32,768 tokens. [Qwen model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B/blob/2fc06364715b967f1860aea9cf38778875588b17/README.md#best-practices) · [Index settings](../../../../settings/qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking.json) · [Index settings at 0.6](../../../../settings/qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking-t0.6.json) · [Authors’ protocol](https://github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs/blob/4d09ba6d6a7e891dd8063974dfe81b3d78e3494f/exp8_math/code/evaluate.py)

At **temperature 0.6** the Index reproduces the authors’ result. Over all 197 items, correct final answers rose from **7.6% to 48.7%**, a **41.1 percentage-point** gain (95% interval 34.2 to 48.0). The authors reported **7.9% to 48.1%**, a **40.2-point** gain. Token-limit hits fell from **92.4% to 34.5%**. The authors reported **93.0% to 33.1%**. [Index base run at 0.6](../../../../../models/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B/runs/math500-n197.qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking-t0.6.bf16.rikiyat-thinking-loops-v1/run.json) · [Index patch run at 0.6](../../../../../models/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B/rikiyat-thinking-loops/v1/runs/math500-n197.qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking-t0.6.bf16/run.json) · [Authors’ results](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/#make-a-language-model-better-at-math)

At **temperature 1.0** the gain is much smaller. In the first **110 filed pairs**, correct final answers rose from **20.0% to 27.3%** and token-limit hits fell from **80.0% to 64.5%**. On the same 110 items at 0.6, the base answered 8.2% and the patched model 57.3%. The base hit the cap more often at 0.6 than at 1.0, and the patched model less often. Every correct Index grade at either temperature came from a reply that finished within the cap. [Index base run](../../../../../models/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B/runs/math500-n197.qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking.bf16.rikiyat-thinking-loops-v1/run.json) · [Index patch run](../../../../../models/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B/rikiyat-thinking-loops/v1/runs/math500-n197.qwen3.5-0.8b-thinking.bf16/run.json)

Other differences remain. The Index used one sample per item on a frozen 197-item subset. The authors averaged four samples per problem across all 500. The inference setups also differed. The Index grades the final boxed answer after thinking closes. The authors’ scorer also searches unfinished replies for a boxed answer.
